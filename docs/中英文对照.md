# 2021 Asia and Pacific Mathematical Contest in Modeling
# 2021 年亚太地区大学生数学建模竞赛

## Problem C
## C 题

# Construction of Ecological Conservation and Assessment of Its Impact on Environment
# 生态保护区的建设及其对环境影响的评估

---

### Background | 题目背景

**English:**

Adhere to the concept that lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets, China insists on respecting for, being harmony with and protecting nature, giving high priority to conserving resources, protecting the environment and letting nature restore itself, implementing sustainable development strategies, improving the overall coordination mechanism in the field of ecological civilization, building an ecological civilization system, promoting the transformation of economic and social development toward comprehensive green growth and building a beautiful country. With the help of the Chinese government, China’s Saihanba Tree Farm has recovered from the desert and has now become an eco-friendly and green farm with the stable sand prevention function.

**中文：**

秉持“绿水青山就是金山银山”的理念，中国坚持尊重自然、顺应自然、保护自然，坚持节约资源和保护环境的基本国策，坚持让自然生态休养生息，实施可持续发展战略，完善生态文明领域的统筹协调机制，构建生态文明制度体系，推动经济社会发展全面绿色转型，建设美丽中国。

在中国政府的支持下，中国塞罕坝林场从昔日的荒漠逐渐恢复，如今已经发展成为一座具有稳定防沙功能、生态环境良好的绿色林场。

---

**English:**

Since 1962, 369 young people with an average age under 24 have come to this wasteland filled with yellow sand. From then on, they dedicated their lives here, advanced wave upon wave, to planting seeds in the sand and planting green in the crevices of the stones, like nails fastening millions of acres of forest on the wasteland. Planting trees to fix the sand and conserve water sources, they build a green barrier to block wind and sand. Today, the forest coverage in Saihanba area has reached 80%. It supplies Beijing and Tianjin with 137 million cubic meters of clean water each year, sequesters 747,000 tons of carbon, and releases 545,000 tons of oxygen.

**中文：**

自 1962 年以来，369 名平均年龄不足 24 岁的年轻人来到这片黄沙遍布的荒原。从那时起，他们便将自己的青春和生命奉献于此，一代接着一代，在沙地上播撒种子，在石缝中种植绿色植被，如同一颗颗钉子，将数百万亩森林牢牢固定在这片荒原之上。

他们通过植树造林来固沙和涵养水源，逐步建立起一道抵御风沙的绿色屏障。如今，塞罕坝地区的森林覆盖率已达到 80%。每年为北京和天津提供 1.37 亿立方米的洁净水资源，吸收 74.7 万吨二氧化碳，并释放 54.5 万吨氧气。

---

**English:**

With over a half century’s struggle, the world’s largest artificial forest was built on the earth of Saihanba. Expanding afforestation of 1.12 million mu with more than 400 million trees, the builders created a green sea on the plateau wasteland which is 400 kilometers north of Beijing.

**中文：**

经过半个多世纪的不懈奋斗，世界上最大的人工林在塞罕坝这片土地上建成。通过扩大造林面积，累计完成 112 万亩造林，种植树木超过 4 亿株。建设者们在这片位于北京以北 400 公里的高原荒原上，创造了一片绿色的海洋。

---

**English:**

On the one hand, there is the historical mission that “civilization will develop followed with zoology thriving”. On the other hand, there are new issues encountered on the road of green development. Therefore, Saihanba people now have a higher goal, which is to restore the ecology. Since the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China, they have successively launched three major projects, namely, afforestation, natural improvement of artificial forests, and near-naturalization cultivation of natural forests. They have tried to make artificial forests closer to natural ones.

**中文：**

一方面，塞罕坝肩负着“生态兴则文明兴”的历史使命；另一方面，在绿色发展的道路上，也面临着新的问题与挑战。

因此，如今的塞罕坝建设者有了更高的目标，即恢复生态系统。自中国共产党第十八次全国代表大会以来，塞罕坝先后启动了三大重点工程，分别是人工造林、人工林自然化改造以及天然林近自然化培育。

通过这些工程，他们努力使人工林逐步向自然森林生态系统靠近。

# Questions | 问题

**English:**

Please build the mathematical models by your team and answer the following questions:

**中文：**

请建立相应的数学模型，并回答以下问题：

---

## Question 1 | 问题一

**English:**

Saihanba plays an important role in resisting wind and sand, protecting environment, maintaining ecological balance and stability, etc. Please select appropriate indicators, collect relevant data, and build the evaluation model for the impact of Saihanba on the ecological environment, in order to quantitatively evaluate the impact on the environment after the restoration of Saihanba; that is, to comparatively analyze the environmental conditions before and after the Saihanba restoration.

**中文：**

塞罕坝在抵御风沙、保护环境、维持生态平衡与稳定等方面发挥着重要作用。

请选取适当的评价指标，收集相关数据，并建立塞罕坝对生态环境影响的评价模型，从而定量评估塞罕坝生态修复工程对环境产生的影响，即对比分析塞罕坝生态修复前后的环境状况。

---

## Question 2 | 问题二

**English:**

The restoration of Saihanba Tree Farm has played an important role in resisting sandstorms for Beijing. Please select appropriate indicators and collect relevant data to build a mathematical model for evaluating the Saihanba’s impact on Beijing’s ability on sandstorm resistance, and quantitatively evaluate Saihanba's role in resisting sandstorms in Beijing.

**中文：**

塞罕坝林场的生态修复在帮助北京抵御沙尘暴方面发挥了重要作用。

请选取适当的评价指标，收集相关数据，建立数学模型，以评估塞罕坝对北京抵御沙尘暴能力的影响，并定量评估塞罕坝在北京防沙治沙方面发挥的作用。

---

## Question 3 | 问题三

**English:**

Suppose we plan to extend the ecological protection model of Saihanba to the whole country, please build a mathematical model and collect relevant data, to determine which geographical locations in China need to build ecological areas (ie. Ecological Reservation), and fix the number or scale of ecological areas to be built; moreover, to evaluate its impact on achieving China’s carbon neutral target.

**中文：**

假设我们计划将塞罕坝的生态保护模式推广至全国，请建立数学模型并收集相关数据，以确定中国哪些地理区域需要建设生态保护区（即 Ecological Reservation），并确定拟建设生态保护区的数量或规模。

此外，还需要评估这些生态保护区的建设对实现中国碳中和目标的影响。

---

## Question 4 | 问题四

**English:**

China’s Saihanba ecological protection model has made an example for the Asia-Pacific region. Please choose another country from the Asia-Pacific region to establish a mathematical model and collect relevant data, and then discuss which geographical locations in this country need to build an ecological area (ie. Ecological Reservation), as well as determine the number or scale of ecological areas to be built; moreover, to evaluate its impact on absorbing greenhouse gases and mitigating carbon emissions.

**中文：**

中国塞罕坝生态保护模式为亚太地区树立了典范。

请从亚太地区选择另一个国家，建立数学模型并收集相关数据，进而讨论该国哪些地理区域需要建设生态保护区（即 Ecological Reservation），并确定拟建设生态保护区的数量或规模。

此外，还需要评估生态保护区的建设对温室气体吸收以及减少碳排放的影响。

---

## Question 5 | 问题五

**English:**

Please write a non-technical report to the Asia-Pacific Mathematical Contest in Modeling Organizing Committee (APMCM), describing your models, and proposing feasible plans and suggestions for building ecological reservation.

**中文：**

请向亚太地区大学生数学建模竞赛组委会（APMCM）提交一份非技术性报告，介绍所建立的数学模型，并针对生态保护区的建设提出切实可行的规划方案和建议。

# Tips | 提示

**English:**

In the process of building the models, you can consider condition of the existing ecological forests in China and other Asia-Pacific regions; the requirement of growth environment for different trees (that is, the specific area for certain tree); how to balance the layout of ecological forest land, economic development land and industrial land; and whether the aimed geographical area has enough land available to develop the ecological reservation.

**中文：**

在建立数学模型的过程中，可以考虑以下因素：

1. 中国及其他亚太地区现有生态林的基本状况。

2. 不同树种对生长环境的要求，即特定树种适宜种植的具体区域。

3. 如何平衡生态林用地、经济发展用地和工业用地之间的空间布局。

4. 目标地理区域是否拥有足够的可用土地来建设生态保护区。
